# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith, argue with the
idea rather than the person, and accept that a maintainer's "no" is an answer
rather than an opening.

Specifically, these are not welcome here: harassment of any kind, personal or
political attacks, demeaning or sexual language, publishing anyone's private
information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem by contacting a maintainer privately — open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/UrsaCode/oilprice/security/advisories/new),
which is the one reporting channel here that is not public, and say at the top
that it is a conduct report rather than a vulnerability. If the person you need
to report is a maintainer, use GitHub's own
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow instead.

Reports are read by the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered. We will
not discuss a report publicly without the reporter's agreement.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and pull
requests that do not meet the standard above, and may block accounts. Where the
reason is not obvious we will say what it was.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, shortened to the parts a project this size actually applies.
