What a litre costs

Every country the scrapers reached in the latest run, converted to US dollars at the exchange rate captured in that same run. The dashed line is what the crude in a litre costs, and everything to the right of it is refining, freight, duty, levy and margin.

Which crude, though. Brent is North Sea and WTI is American, but a refinery in Karachi or Dhaka is buying Arab Light from Saudi Arabia and Murban from the UAE, and those cargoes are priced off Dubai. The sellers do not all charge the same, so the line moves depending on whose barrel you mean.
